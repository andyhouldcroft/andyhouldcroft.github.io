Quantitative ecology for human-wildlife coexistence

Andy Houldcroft is a quantitative ecologist based in Cornwall. His research aims to develop and apply cutting-edge methods to monitor and conserve endangered species. He works as part of the Cantanhez Chimpanzee Project and the Centre for Environmental Intelligence at the University of Exeter.
